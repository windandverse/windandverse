---
title: "Use Less"
Author: "Roméon"
---

In a room full of babies       
You would be most innocent       
or so I used to think       
But since you led a mutiny      
To devour me from within       
I don’t know what to think     
Even my pain is confused      
The light of your betrayal     
Blinds my decomposition      
Of the love we used to know    
While I’m rotting away     
You broke my shell      
Made me slug     
Dropped me in space      
How do you go from opium     
to Sodium     
Cyanide       
You were poison all along     
I curse the day     
The day I learnt how to hope     
I should have known     
you stole my heart      
and I locked you up      
in my cells, but     
now my DNA is MIA     
for I never recognised       
the compromises      
I made my disguise     
How I wish I were useless    
Maybe then you would    
use less of me     
as I am     
Defeated. Depleted     
Have the mercy     
to leave me for dead     

~ Roméon 🧟 ~
